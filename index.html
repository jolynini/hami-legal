Privacy Policy

This explains what Hami collects, why, and who ever sees it — written the same way the app talks to you: plainly, and without surprises.

In plain language
  • Hami collects what it needs to notice changes in your routine and remind you about medications and appointments — never more.
  • Nothing is shared with family until you turn that sharing on, category by category.
  • Hami does not sell your data, and does not use it to advertise to you or anyone else.
  • Hami never diagnoses you. It notices, it asks, and — only if you want — it connects you with someone you already trust.
Section 1

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Hami mobile app, developed by Joel Orón Valiente together with Aylin Ourang ("we," "us"). It applies to the elder using Hami and to any family member or caregiver they invite as a trusted contact.

Section 3

How it's used

Your routine and check-in data are compared only against your own history — a rolling average of your normal patterns — never against anyone else's. When Hami flags something as unusual, it always shows the specific reasons (for example, "activity much lower than usual" or "morning medication not confirmed"); it never produces an unexplained score.

What Hami never does with this data
  • Never diagnoses a medical condition or suggests a medication change.
  • Never declares a medical emergency on its own — it only ever offers to connect you with a contact you've already approved.
  • Never uses your data to build an advertising profile, and never sells it.
Section 4

Family sharing & consent

Nothing is visible to a family member until you invite them and turn sharing on for that category, in Settings. You choose, independently:

  • Whether they can see medication status, check-in status, activity level, appointments, or your full timeline.
  • Which alerts reach them, and how long Hami waits for you to respond before it does.

An invited contact only gains access once they accept your invitation. You can revoke access at any time from Settings.

Section 6

Voice & the assistant

Hami only listens while you're actively pressing and holding the microphone button — never in the background. Medication, appointment, and HELP questions are always answered by fixed, predictable rules, on-device; only everything else is passed to Gemini, and even then, only the words you spoke and the minimum context needed to answer (like your first name and medication schedule) — never your location, contacts, or health history beyond that.

Section 7

Retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, so your routine baseline and health timeline stay meaningful. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us below; we'll delete it within 30 days, except where a short backup-retention window applies.

Section 8

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is enforced by row-level database policies — your data is scoped to your account, and a family member's access is scoped to exactly the categories you've turned on, checked on every request rather than assumed.

Section 9

Your choices & rights

  • Access & correction — review and edit your profile, medications, and settings directly in the app.
  • Sharing controls — turn any shared category on or off at any time.
  • Deletion — request full account deletion (see Retention).
  • Portability — request an export of your data by contacting us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA. [Add region-specific detail once you know your user base.]

Section 10

Children

Hami is designed for adult use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your region).

Section 11

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to how we handle your data, we'll notify you in the app before it takes effect, not just by quietly updating this page.
